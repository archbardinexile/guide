###### Summary

The Sylvan Kingdoms are a cluster of unaffiliated territories that reside on a continental island erde-north of [[Atada and Atado|Atada]]. Despite their collective name, not all territories in the Sylvan Kingdoms are monarchist. In fact, many are staunchly anarchist, reflecting the chaotic nature of the Feywild. Settlements vary greatly in culture, architecture and how closely they revere their Feywild heritage. Much of the landscape is abundant with flora and fauna from the Feywild, with the densest jungle originating in [[#Parth Brenin]].

Visitors to the Sylvan Kingdoms often find that sensations, both sensory and emotional, are heightened. Smells are stronger and colours more vivid, while shadows are darker and impulses harder to control. Strong emotions could even alter the landscape as it would in the Feywild; despondent travelers might leave a trail of wilting flowers, while carefree animals prance alongside cheerful adventurers.

## Parth Brenin

Parth Brenin is a coastal island settlement on the Sylvan Kingdoms' north-eastern border. It is the home of [[Anfarwol]]'s Portal, which grants the land magical properties one might find in the Feywild. The portal is housed inside Caer Wyllt, the fortress home of Golden King [[Marwol]], who acts as its custodian, granting passage to and from the Feywild where appropriate. Marwol commands a legion of expert eladrin warriors who protect the fortress and the portal.

## Morte-de-Márcia

Known as Puertofio prior to the withdrawal of occupying [[Kingdom of Diamante|Diamante]] forces, Morte-de-Márcia lies on the Sylvan Kingdoms' southern coast, in a coveted location ideal for international trading. Much of Morte-de-Márcia's population consists of avian races who migrated away from taxing Feywild environs in search of fresh sea air. Morte-de-Márcia is named after the execution of Countess Márcia Antunes Siqueira, Puertofio's governess during the Diamante occupation.

#### The Occupation of Puertofio (808 PO)

Facing a financial crisis, the Kingdom of Diamante sought to establish relations with Puertofio, an ideal trading hub on the Sylvan Kingdoms' southern coast. Diamante were initially welcomed by the avian population; they offered modern construction techniques and the industrial infrastructure that had become essential since the arrival of [[Ingenium]] in 748 PO. While Puertofio prospered commercially over a period of eight years, its expansion took its toll on an already overworked population.

In 808 PO, when Countess Márcia flippantly increased taxation to placate her suzerain in Diamante, the people mounted a successful raid on the town's barracks. Inspired by the ongoing war of independence in [[Émeraude Republic|Émeraude]], a sizable mob was able to overpower the Countess' guards and ransack the Siqueira estate. The Countess was taken hostage and escorted to the town square where she was trialled and executed. The town was renamed Morte-de-Márcia in remembrance and a statue erected representing the town's unity.
