Profundus is the largest region at the centre of the [[The Relief/index|Relief]]. It plays host to one of the largest and most populous cities in the Relief, [[Caelum's Landing]], the [[Church of Caelum]]'s capital city. Adventurers and tourists can take advantage of sprawling markets and travel to nearby [[Earthmotes|earthmotes]] on powered cable cars.
