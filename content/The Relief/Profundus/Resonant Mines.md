###### Summary

The Resonant Mines house the largest deposit of [[index#Resonite|resonite]] ever discovered on [[Fio]]. Lying beneath the [[Altum Sea]], it is speculated the mines span the distance from [[Fundus]] to [[Atada and Atado|Atada]]. A crystalline sinkhole protruding from the sea marks the entrance to the mine where barricades keep the sea at bay and scaffolding spirals down to seemingly infinite depths. Scholars speculate that [[Micantrum]] arose from this sinkhole one thousand years ago. Budding and experienced miners alike will always find work here, but the precarious work conditions can often prove taxing, sometimes fatal.
