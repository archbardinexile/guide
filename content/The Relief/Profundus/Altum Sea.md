The Altum Sea stretches far into the uncharted regions of the [[Fio]]. As a result, most denizens of the [[The Relief|Relief]] tend to have at least a rudimentary understanding of seafaring or aviation. The destruction of [[Mundi Eversor]] wrought great storms that filled the cavities left behind after the [[index#The Rising|Rising]], leading many aquatic races to take refuge in its depths.

The largest congregation of sea-folk in the Relief resides at the bottom of the Vena Trench. This mysterious underwater city has been named [[#Abyssus]] by explorers that have until recently only inferred its existence. Not much is known about the city, other than it lies east of [[Mundi Eversor]].

## Abyssus

Expeditions to this underwater city have been met with adversity, trying to combat the pressure of the trench's depths and indeed its protective citizens. In 990 PO, an advanced submarine from [[Ingenium]] captured images of jellyfish-like structures at the bottom of the [[Altum Sea|Vena Trench]]. The submarine was attacked on its return journey by triton riding giant sharks. Fortunately, the crew was able to escape unharmed. The crew, who until that moment had only inferred its existence, named the mysterious underwater city Abyssus.
