###### Summary

The Altum Sea expands across the [[The Relief|Relief]] and into the [[Fio#The Miasma|Miasma]] in every cardinal direction. As a result, most denizens of the Relief tend to have at least a rudimentary understanding of seafaring or aviation. The destruction of [[Mundi Eversor]] wrought great storms that filled the cavities left behind after the [[index#The Rising|Rising]], leading many aquatic races to take refuge in its depths.

## Vena Trench

The Vena Trench is a seafloor trench that spans the divide between [[Profundus]] and [[Discidium]]. The trench is estimated to be 420 miles long, 120 miles wide (at its widest point), and six miles deep (at its deepest point).

### Abyssus

The largest congregation of sea-folk in the Relief resides at the bottom of the Vena Trench. This mysterious underwater city has been named Abyssus by explorers that have until recently only inferred its existence. Not much is known about the city, other than it lies east of [[Mundi Eversor]]. Expeditions to Abyssus have been met with adversity, trying to combat the pressure of the trench's depths and indeed its protective citizens.

#### Abyssus is discovered (990 PO)

 In 990 PO, an advanced submarine from [[Ingenium]] captured images of jellyfish-like structures at the bottom of the Vena Trench. The submarine was attacked on its return journey by triton riding giant sharks. Fortunately, the crew was able to escape unharmed. The crew, who until that moment had only inferred its existence, named the mysterious underwater city Abyssus.
