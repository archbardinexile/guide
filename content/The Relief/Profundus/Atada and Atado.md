###### Summary

Atada and Atado are twinned [[Fio#Earthmotes|earthmotes]] west of [[Fundus]] celebrated for their natural beauty and rich gemstone deposits. Atada, the larger of the two earthmotes is home to the [[Kingdom of Diamante]], a feudal nation to which the realms of Atada and Atado have sworn fealty. Ruled by a caelumite monarchy, Diamante has enjoyed good relations with the [[Church of Caelum]] since its founding in 241 PO by [[Rex Sol Orbis]]. The current monarch is Queen [[Carina Diamante IV]].
