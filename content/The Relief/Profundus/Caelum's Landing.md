###### Summary

Caelum's Landing is the capital city of the most prevalent nation in the [[The Relief|Relief]]—the [[Church of Caelum]]. At the centre of [[Fundus]], this modernised city serves as the beating economic and cultural heart of the Relief. Caelum's Landing and its neighbouring settlements are home to nearly one million people of countless races and cultures. Once known as Anfang, the city rose to power when [[Caelum Sol Orbis]] ascended to shield his people from the growing [[Fio#The Miasma|Miasma]] one thousand years ago.

## The Oratory

The Oratory stands at the centre of Caelum's Landing, a great spire serving as the seat of the Church of Caelum and residence of the Exarch, [[Aura Sol Orbis]]. Here, hopeful acolytes train to become clerics in service of Caelum and other good-aligned deities. A traditional open-door policy has allowed citizens from all backgrounds to avail themselves of the Oratory's services since its construction more than one thousand years ago.

## Saviour's Ward

Surrounding the Oratory, Saviour's Ward is the largest of Caelum's Landing's six wards, where one can find accommodation and public services to sate any need. Inns always have beds and taverns always have beer. Street carts can be summoned to travel anywhere on Fundus and [[index#Resonite|resonite]]-powered street lighting will illuminate your journey at night.

## Craftsman's Ward

East of the Oratory, Craftsman's Ward is the powerhouse behind Caelum's Landing's industry. At its centre, factories work tireless to product machines and vehicles powered by resonite and airships can be seen departing from vast airfields. The headquarters of the [[The Ministry#Inland Guard|Inland Guard]] can be found on the border to Saviour's Ward.

## Ministerial Houses

North-east of the Oratory, the Ministerial Houses play host to the nation's government—the [[The Ministry|Ministry]]; a council of elected ministers representing the many provinces under the Church's jurisdiction. Their Chairman, [[Emil Hartmann]] serves as adjudicator and liases with the Exarch to sanction new laws.

## Merchant's Ward

South of the Oratory, Merchant's Ward is where shoppers, tourists and adventurers can find the stalls of travelling merchants, the workshops of artisans, and skilled alchemists who can both heal and invigorate the most daring of adventurers. This thriving marketplaces regularly holds the Guilds Exhibition, a quarterly event when guilds and entrepreneurs from all over the Relief gather to recruit members and promote their business.
