###### Summary

Nox is a fiercely independent and somewhat impoverished [[Fio#Earthmotes|earthmote]] founded by the reclusive silver dragon, [[Nox (Dragon)|Nox]]. Despite appearances, Nox does not serve as the commune's leader, though he occasionally assumes the role of mentor. Nox's treacherous altitude allows its citizens to live under a perpetual night sky, illuminated only by a halo of daylight and ribbons of chromatic aurora.

Only a small proportion of the earthmote is populated. Wooden longhouses strengthened against bitter winds serve as the primary homes for Nox's citizens. When winter arrives, citizens must take shelter with the silver dragon in the belly of the earthmote. The cold climate also makes cultivating the land difficult. Nox is home to many avian and draconic races and those that prefer colder climates.

#### Nox arrives in Fio (92 PO)

In 92 PO, Nox emerged from a ring of aurora in the sky skew-north-east of the [[Sylvan Kingdoms]]. The silver dragon, Nox, who had blessed the earthmote with his namesake, flew down to [[Caelum's Landing]] in search of help. When confronted by the nascent [[Church of Caelum]]'s zealotry, Nox returned home intent on securing a self-reliant future for his people in wintry isolation.
