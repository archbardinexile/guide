North of [[Mallia]], this archipelago of diminutive isles bears a colloquial name since the [[Factions and Characters/Combe Shipping and Courier Agency/index|CSCA]] monopolised trade in the region. Despite the [[Factions and Characters/Church of Caelum/index|Church of Caelum]]'s influence, the Manyshells have become a reliable refuge for pirates, cultists, adrenaline junkies and other counter cultures.
