Contritus is the easternmost region of the [[The Relief|Relief]]. The region is divided by the far-reaching archipelagos of the [[Manyshells]] and [[Gwerdowra]]. To the south-east, the enormous jungle [[Fio#Earthmotes|earthmote]] [[Mallia]] appears to drain the sea with curtain-like vines that veil its shores.
