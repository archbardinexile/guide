###### Summary

Eastern Gwerdowra, or simply Gwerdowra, is an archipelago in [[Contritus]], south of [[Primal Antiqua]]. When the eastern islands protested against the [[Combe Shipping and Courier Agency|CSCA]]'s oppressive trade influence in the [[Manyshells]], the islands of Gwerdowra banded together to form a coalition government with [[#Anchora]] as its capital. Having thwarted the CSCA's expansion, their nascent military, the [[Free Trade Navy]], now police the eastern seas.
