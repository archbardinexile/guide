Contritus is the easternmost region of the [[The Relief/index|Relief]]. The region is broken by the far-reaching fjords and archipelagos of the [[Manyshells]]. To the south-east, the enormous jungle [[earthmotes|earthmote]] [[Mallia]] appears to drain the sea with the curtain-like vines that veil its shores.
