###### Summary

Tamlo is an [[Fio#Earthmotes|earthmote]] west of [[Atada and Atado|Atada]] populated primarily by diminutive bestial races. Ruled by the nefarious [[ratfolk]] king [[Skagra]], non-ratfolk races tolerate second-class citizenship in a gothic city of depravity and pestilence. A notoriously insular and paranoid city, Tamlo meticulously polices trade and migration. Many non-ratfolk resort to organised insurgency, hoping end the hoarder king's rule.

#### Tamlo arrives in Fio (972 PO)

First contact with Tamlo was made in 972 PO when the earthmote appeared west of Atada in a cloud of black smoke. The [[Church of Caelum]]'s ambassadors were denied entry for several weeks until summoned by King Skagra. The king seemed most interested in [[index#Resonite|resonite]], a gemstone apparently unique to [[Fio]] which powered impressive machines on [[Fundus]] and [[Ingenium]]. Despite the ambassadors' warnings, Tamlo launched an expedition into the [[Fio#The Miasma|Miasma]] in search of resonite for themselves. Months later, the expedition was reported missing. Notably, the [[Church of Caelum#The Inquisition|Inquisition]] was founded soon after.
