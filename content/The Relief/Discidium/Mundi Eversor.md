###### Summary

Mundi Eversor was the name of the colossus said to have been created by [[Tenebrae]] to destroy the world. The [[index#The Rising|Ascensus in Caelum]] teaches that 1,000 years ago, [[Caelum Sol Orbis]] did battle with Eversor and in its destruction the [[Fio#The Miasma|Miasma]] was released from its dark, crystalline heart.

Erde-south of [[Atada and Atado|Atada]], the derelict remains of the ancient colossus can still be seen protruding from the [[Altum Sea]]. Great metallic blades curve into an enormous rib cage formation, large enough to fit [[Fundus]] inside. Scholars have discovered further remains in the depths of the Altum sea and estimate the colossus was five times the size of Fundus.

The landmass is too unstable to house a settlement, but expeditions commonly venture to Eversor hoping to chart the expanse of its remains. This is complicated by the magical pressures that surround Eversor, making it difficult to cast magic within its confines. Eversor's pivotal role in history occasionally draws pilgrims from wayward cults who search for enlightenment in the stillness.
