Mundi Eversor was the name of the colossus said to have been created by [[Tenebrae]] to destroy the world. The [[index#The Rising|Ascensus in Caelum]] teaches that 1,000 years ago, [[Caelum Sol Orbis]] did battle with Eversor and in its destruction [[Fio]] was released from its dark, crystalline heart.

To the erde-south-west, the derelict remains of the ancient colossus can still be seen protruding from the [[Altum Sea]]. Great metallic blades curve into an enormous rib cage formation, large enough to fit [[Fundus]] inside. Scholars have discovered further remains in the depths of the Altum sea and estimate the colossus to have been ten times the size of Fundus. The landmass is too unstable to house a settlement, but here you may find expeditions looking to further chart the expanse of Eversor's remains, or pilgrims from wayward cults searching for enlightenment amidst the magical energies that linger there to this day.
