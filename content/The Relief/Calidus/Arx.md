###### Summary

Constructed around Lake Fossa on the south western border, Arx is famous for its abundance of culture and trade. Its growth in the last century is second only to [[Caelum's Landing]], and can be attributed to merchants advertising and contributing to its wealth of food, art and community spirit. A frequent saying in [[Calidus]] is, "A friend set in sandstone," referring to the city's sandstone architecture. The saying may be derived from tales of travellers almost succumbing to Calidus' harsh desert environs before generous Arxmen offered them shelter. Arx's most wealthy patron and leading governor is [[Shadal ibn Lakhim]], who has invested much of his fortune into developing Arx into a utopian refuge amidst the sands of Calidus.
