###### Summary



#### Creed's Hollow arrives on Fio (996 PO)

In 996 PO, Creed's Hollow emerged from the [[Fio#The Miasma|Miasma]]'s western border in a ring of hellfire, as if torn from *Avernus* and thrown by *Zariel* herself. The [[Fio#Earthmotes|earthmote]] cast a menacing shadow over [[Madanau]] as it flew east, raining ash and bloodthirsty fiends down on the peaceful oasis. Madanau's citizens were rushed to shelters while clerics from the [[Church of Caelum]] were dispatched to repel the invaders.

With the fiends defeated, monks from the [[Spirit Temple]] were able to arrest the earthmote and attend to its displaced denizens. The Church's ambassadors were met by a village of tiefling farmers who, until recently, had been living in seclusion in Avernus, free of Zariel's rule. Creed's Hollow now enjoys healthy relations with the Church and trades in agricultural produce native to the *Nine Hells*.

Though Madanau weathered the attack, the oasis at its centre was damaged by a shard of hellish black rock that fell from Creed's Hollow. The oasis' source, a portal to the elemental plane of water, continues to produce blights of tainted water and spoiled fish as a result of the attack. This has lead many of Madanau's citizens to leave and seek refuge on [[Caelum's Landing]]. Despite this, Madanau's remaining citizens have developed an unparalleled community and work ethic. With help from the [[Dawnroot Druids]], they have been able to stabilise the portal and develop measures to prevent or mitigate future blights.
