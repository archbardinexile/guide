###### Summary

Madanau has been revealed by scholars to be one of the first [[Fio#Earthmotes|earthmotes]] to arrive on [[Fio]] during [[Fio#Caelum's Light|Caelum's Light]]. Estimated to be nearly 800 years old, this desert earthmote lies far erde-west of [[Caelum's Landing]], treacherously close to the [[Fio#The Miasma|Miasma]]. Under the administration of [[Sana Sol Orbis]], Madanau has made its name as a valued hub of trade, able to grow and export exotic fruits and spices that cannot be found anywhere else in the [[The Relief|Relief]]. The grand, circular oasis at Madanau's centre hosts a portal to the elemental plane of water, providing an abundance of fresh water and delicious fish.

#### Madanau arrives on Fio (204 PO)

In 204 PO, the desert oasis Madanau appeared erde-west of [[Fundus]] in a whirlwind of sand. Initially enveloped by the Miasma, the enormous emerald dragon, [[Vulcan]], emerged from his dormancy beneath [[Montanum]] to push Madanau beyond the border with his hurricane-like breath. Denizens who saw the dragon before he returned to his slumber have passed on the tale to future generations.
