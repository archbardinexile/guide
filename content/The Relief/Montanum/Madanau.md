###### Summary

Madanau has been revealed by scholars to be one of the first [[Fio]] to arrive in [[Fio]] during [[Fio]]. Estimated to be nearly 800 years old, this desert earthmote lies far erde-west of [[Caelum's Landing]], treacherously close to the [[Fio]]. Under the administration of [[Sana Sol Orbis]], Madanau has made its name as a valued hub of trade, able to grow and export exotic fruits and spices that cannot be found anywhere else in the [[The Relief|Relief]]. The grand, circular oasis at Madanau's centre hosts a portal to the elemental plane of water, providing an abundance of fresh water and delicious fish.

###### History

In 204 PO, the desert oasis Madanau appeared erde-west of [[Fundus]] in a whirlwind of sand. Initially enveloped by the [[Fio]], the enormous emerald dragon, [[Vulcan]], emerged from his dormancy beneath [[Montanum|Montanum]] to push Madanau beyond the border with his hurricane-like breath. Denizens who saw the dragon before he returned to his slumber have passed on the tale to future generations.

## Creed's Hollow

![[Creed's Hollow]]

Though Madanau weathered the attack, the oasis at its centre was damaged by a shard of hellish black rock that fell from Creed's Hollow. The oasis' source, a portal to the elemental plane of water, continues to produce blights of tainted water and spoiled fish as a result of the attack. This has lead many of Madanau's citizens to leave and seek refuge on [[Caelum's Landing]]. Despite this, Madanau's remaining citizens have developed an unparalleled community and work ethic. With help from the [[Dawnroot Druids]], they have been able to stabilise the portal and develop measures to prevent or mitigate future blights.
