###### Summary

West of the [[Caelum's Landing#The Oratory|Oratory]], Caelum’s Landing University, or CLU, is the largest educational institution in the [[The Relief|Relief]]. Its main campus on [[Fundus]] is large enough to warrant a ward of its own. Here you can take crash courses to learn new spells, gain new skills and new tool proficiencies. Or, if you wish to carve out a different path for yourself, you may enrol at the university and learn all the essential skills you need to make a fresh start. The university's famous library and observatory are always open to the public. Adventurers looking to travel to nearby [[Fio#Earthmotes|earthmotes]] must visit the Glider Proficiency Centre to pass an assessment before they can pilot a glider of their own.

## Glider Proficiency Centre

Those wishing to explore the skies without the aid of public transportation must visit Caelum's Landing University to gain their glider proficiency. Gliders are the simplest and cheapest way to traverse the skies between earthmotes, and are as commonplace as the humble bicycle. They come in single and two-seat variants. Some larger gliders even come equipped with [[index#Resonite|resonance engines]] to travel longer distances.

> [!warning]
> A word to the wise: most personal gliders are not built to handle strong winds and stormy weather. I strongly recommend hiring an airship for more taxing journeys.
