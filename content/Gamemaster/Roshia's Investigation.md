- **Inceptum 4th.** Roshia attempts to contact Gomruss who is in the Miasma. "The Inquisition are making a move."
- **Inceptum 13th.** Roshia attempts to contact Gomruss again. "Camren has been arrested and the Inquisition are interested in the Dawnroot Druids."
- **Inceptum 15th.** The Pillar is activated. The Inquisition attack the Dawnroot Druids. The druids share their memory stone with Roshia before she escapes. Roshia contacts Gomruss. "I'm looking for a way out of Caelum's Landing. I witnessed the Inquisition attack the Dawnroot Druids!" Roshia is captured by the Inquisition and her memory erased. She returns to Arx.
- **Inceptum 24th.** Firien speaks with the Sol Orbis family about the attack on the druids. They believe the Inquisition was looking for Tristis' letter.
- **Inceptum 25th.** Gomruss contacts Roshia, who seems to have no memory of the attack. She requests he return her pocket watch to him.
