According to the Prosperian Calendar, introduced by [[Prospero Sol Orbis]] in 111 PO, there are twelve 28-day months in a year and seven days in a week. The [[The Relief|Relief]] experiences a leap year every four years when [[Fio]] and [[Auga]] align with a planar portal. This event is known as [[Caelum's Light]].
