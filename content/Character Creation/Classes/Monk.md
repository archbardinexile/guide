## Monastic Tradition—Way of the Spirit Temple

Monks of the Spirit Temple seek a deep connection to nature through the study of wild beasts and their associated elements. This allows them to terraform new lands and arrest dangerous rogue [[Fio#Earthmotes|earthmotes]].

### 3rd Level

#### Druidic

At 3rd level, when you adopt this tradition, you learn Druidic, the secret language of druids. You can speak the language and use it to leave hidden messages. You and others who know this language automatically spot such a message. Others spot the message's presence with a successful DC 15 Wisdom (Perception) check but can't decipher it without magic.

#### Animal Guide

At 3rd level, you choose an animal guide and gain its feature. You must study the behaviour of an animal in the wild to use as inspiration for your martial arts. Your guide might be an animal related to those listed here but more appropriate to your homeland. For example, you could choose an eagle or vulture in place of a crane.

**Bear.** When you spend one or more ki points, you gain resistance to all damage except psychic damage until the start of your next turn.

**Crane.** Deflect missiles now affects ranged weapon attacks targeting allies within 5 feet of you.

**Elephant.** You learn two cantrips and one 1st-level ritual spell of your choice from the druid spell list.

**Tiger.** When you use flurry of blows, add your wisdom modifier to the damage it deals on a hit. You can choose whether your unarmed strikes deal bludgeoning or slashing damage.

**Wolf.** Whenever you make an attack roll or a saving throw while two or more allies are within 5 feet of you, can roll a d4 and add the number rolled to the attack roll or saving throw.
