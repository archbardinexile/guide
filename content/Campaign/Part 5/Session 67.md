As the sun creeps steadily toward the horizon, Gomruss reaches out to Roshia. The professor urges the party to seek out a shadar-kai by the name of Thrace at the Calidus Archaeological Society—he could help them traverse the desert. Walking the streets, the Six of Steel draw the attention of two civilians, who gasp when they spot Gomruss. Blythe secretly engages the pair, claiming to be notorious detective Robin Holmes. According to the civilians, several years ago, Gomruss was put on trial for murdering his wife! Though he was acquitted, many thought he was guilty.

The society building offers a welcome respite from the heat. Thrace steps forward when the receptionist calls his name. The grizzled man is dressed to combat the harsh sands of Calidus; customised tools hang from his belt and backpack. He recognises the Six of Steel by their reputation. First, he recommends the party purchase more appropriate clothes for desert environs. Next, he offers to entreat the services of local githzechi traders whose sand skimmers could get them to the Tomb of Darkhalijz much faster.

Outside the market, the Six of Steel are met by a vigilante mob. Toting improvised weapons, they clearly intend to exile Gomruss or worse. Firien steps forward, creating an intimidating thunderclap to silence the crowd. As a cleric of Bahamut, they declare appropriate justice to have already been served years ago. When the mob become ambivalent, a unique, shrill voice protests. A golden dragonborn in lavish robes pushes through the crowd to join the Six of Steel. The mob disperses at his request.

The dragonborn introduces himself as Fuxuc Cis Bandorul, a local councillor and cousin to Gomruss. Apparently, Fuxuc helped Gomruss find accommodation when he was first accepted at the University of Arx. Much to Fuxuc's surprise, Gomruss does not recall. Regardless, the councillor offers his family home as safe haven for the Six of Steel should the need arise. Gomruss thanks his cousin, but insists the party should leave for the Tomb of Darkhalijz as soon as possible.

Content to not ask questions, Thrace leads the party to a warehouse on the city's outskirts. There they find a crew of githzechi trading minerals for provisions. Thrace greets Qemoc, apparently a dubious acquaintance of his, and requests passage to the Tomb of Darkhalijz. Qemoc seems hesitant, claiming the tomb is a place of ill omen. The githzechi suggests his crew might brave the journey if Thrace were to acquire some spice for them. Inferring Qemoc's unscrupulous desire, Gomruss is able to barter for passage using the joose-infused potions he confiscated from the smugglers in the Manyshells.

### Calidus Desert

Though savage in appearance, the sand skimmers are light and manoeuvrable. Each boasts a vertical wing that allows the githzechi to sail the sands as if they were at sea. To traverse difficult terrain, each skimmer is equipped with a resonite engine that powers four great furrowed wheels. Thrace announces the imminent arrival of a sandstorm—they would have to rely on dead reckoning to reach their destination. After an hour, Thrace raises a fist to halt the convoy. Trudging through the sand, he finds himself looking down over the edge of a narrow canyon. They had arrived.

### Tomb of Darkhalijz

Abseiling into the canyon, the Six of Steel find Roshia and a tiefling colleague at the tomb's recently excavated entrance. Roshia introduces Dayal, an archaeology student here on an internship. Dayal's feline eyes and frantic tail betray their survivor's guilt. Bantonio asks the names of those missing. Roshia reads three names from her notes: Xander Coppermane, an experienced adventurer from Nox; Doctor Gurdeep Darshan, whom Gomruss met at Crowswood Place; and Charis Madizos, a local mercenary.

Arti spots a glimmer of rage in Firien's eyes. Did Roshia say Madizos? The dragonborn remains silent. Before entering the tomb, Roshia tosses Thrace a bag of coins. He weighs the payment in his hands. Satisfied, he departs with a nonchalant salute. While Arti and Bantonio investigate the largest chamber, Gomruss returns Roshia's pocket watch. He explains that although for him the watch shows a time dilation, it is unable to restore his memories—he had blocked them out himself.

Roshia sympathises with his plight and offers to help, first by restoring the memory the Inquisition had apparently stolen from her. She activates the watch and a wave of magic washes over her. Her eyes widen in disbelief as she tries to articulate her confusion.

> "The Inquisition were prepared. They attacked Pen Ffynnon as soon as the Miasma disappeared. The portal the druids protect; it contained a memory stone—the memory of a druid who witnessed Caelum's ascension. The druids shared that memory with me before the Inquisition confiscated it."
> 
> *—Roshia*

Firien's eyes now burn with rage. They had assumed Tristis sent the Inquisition to retrieve his incriminating letter concerning Naal, when truthfully he knew about the memory stone all along. The attack occurred before Tristis took the oath and although he had not disclosed this information since, he had not technically lied. He had circumvented the oath. Roshia rubs her temples as she tries to describe the druid's memory.

> "I saw Caelum surrounded by his saints. They were performing some kind of ritual, channelling radiant magic into him. Someone came to warn Caelum about Mundi Eversor; a woman called... Lao Tan."
> 
> *—Roshia*
