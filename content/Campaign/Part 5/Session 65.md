Darkness. A familiar pressure smothers Guy's ears. He was underwater. Yet he could breathe. The sensation was not unwelcome, peaceful rather. A faint teal light appears in the gloom. Guy's instincts tell him to turn away but he finds himself unable. The light was drawing him in. His heart hastens as he braces himself for the bite of a deep-sea angler. Instead, the strike comes from an emaciated hand riddled with seaweed and barnacles. Its vice grip seals Guy's windpipe as the face of its owner emerges.

> "You cannae get rid of me that easily, Brush."
> 
> *—Bhaldrom Anchorheart*

## 996, Flavum 25th

### The Hissing Hand

Guy wakes with a start. The morning sun had pierced the curtains at his bedside. It had been two weeks since the siege of Farwest, yet his mind still dwelt on its repercussions. Thanks to the Six of Steel, Rose, Subira and Agnes were safe. Bhaldrom Anchorheart had been defeated and Elyse was to be given command of the reclaimed Aubrey once it was seaworthy. Why then did he fear that peace was fleeting? He snatches up the newspaper on his bedside table.



Firien weighed down by responsibility. Hasn't contacted Spiro since the siege.
