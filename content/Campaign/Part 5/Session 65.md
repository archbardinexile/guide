Darkness. A familiar pressure smothers Guy's ears. He was underwater. Yet he could breathe. The sensation was not unwelcome, peaceful rather. A faint teal light appears in the gloom. Guy's instincts tell him to turn away but he finds himself unable. The light was drawing him in. His heart hastens as he braces himself for the bite of a deep-sea angler. Instead, the strike comes from an emaciated hand riddled with seaweed and barnacles. Its vice grip seals Guy's windpipe as the face of its owner emerges.

> "You cannae get rid of me that easily, Brush."
> 
> *—Bhaldrom Anchorheart*

## 996, Flavum 21st

### The Hissing Hand

Guy wakes with a start. The morning sun had pierced the curtains at his bedside. It had been ten days since the siege of Farwest, yet his mind still dwelt on its repercussions. Thanks to the Six of Steel, Rose, Subira and Agnes were safe. Bhaldrom Anchorheart had been defeated and Elyse was to be given a commendation. Elyse had invited Guy to join her crew but he had not yet made a decision. Could he leave the Six of Steel at a time when they were most vulnerable? He snatches up the newspaper on his bedside table.

![[Boatswain's Call Article]]

Bantonio returns to The Hissing Hand with a healthy baked breakfast for the party. Firien had not yet emerged from their room; recent events must still be weighing heavily upon them. Bantonio decides to deliver breakfast to their door. To his delight, Firien invites him inside. After a savouring bite of a bread roll, Firien feels comfortable enough to express their persistent rage. Why must they be the only one responsible for amending the mistakes of a Church they didn't even worship? Despite everything, they had prevented war in the Manyshells and retained Bahamut's blessing. Why then did they still feel like shit?

> "You know, a wise person once told me we are human beings, not human doings."
>
> "Thanks Tony. How's your niece?"
>
> "Oh, terrible; they crack when I stand up."
> 
> *—Bantonio consoles Firien*

Bantonio had spent much of his downtime catching up with Elisa and providing support at her performances. He was thankful to have family in his life again, though Elisa could not yet reveal his existence to anyone. Encouraged by Bantonio's optimism, Firien resolves to buy a tuna at the fish market so they might quell their nerves by baking a delicious meal.

Downstairs, Arti plucks a bundle of flowers from his shoulder to pot yet another houseplant for The Hissing Hand. It was the height of spring and Arti was in full bloom. He had spent much of his downtime sunbathing with Subira and inadvertently decorating all of Sullivanwick. He was sad to see the aged tabaxi return to Caelum's Landing.

At the table, Guy observes Gomruss as the dragonborn munches on his bread roll. He was evidently contemplating something very deeply. Gomruss had not yet revealed the taxing nature of the flashbacks he was having. He could not yet determine if they were mere fantasies or something more sinister. The thought of returning to Arx filled him with dread.
