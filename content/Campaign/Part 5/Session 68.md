Blythe opts to stay behind with Roshia. She warns that a mirror counterpart to each of the party will spawn somewhere in the Plane of Mirrors once they cross the threshold. Dayal offers to join the party. They could tell the difference between the expedition members and their counterparts. Gomruss approaches the portal to meet his reflection. He reaches out, touching his fingertips to those of his reflection, only to find the glass is permeable. With a deep breath, the Six of Steel step through the portal.

### Plane of Mirrors

The path ahead is almost imperceptible. All around, an infinite undulating web of glass shards reflects thousands of locations, as if the party were inside a shattered mirror. Behind them, the vault doors close. When questioned, Darkhalijz explains that his staff could not only banish his enemies to the Plane of Mirrors, but deposit them in a cell fashioned from their most torturous memories. When Darkhalijz admits that he never figured out how to release his victims, Firien grips his throat, silencing him.

Moving forward, Arti spots the Miasma reflected in a small glass shard. Approaching carefully, they equip their lantern flail. When the portal next appears, Arti's lantern appears in the reflection, dispersing the Miasma. Arti sings Saint Lydia's song. As they suspected, a distant beacon of light illuminates the Miasma. Another pillar! Firien takes note of the volcanic terrain beyond the threshold. Could this portal lead to Discidium? The pair agree to return here once the expedition are safe.

The glass shards become larger as the party stray further from the vault doors. Up ahead, Dayal spots a reflection of Nox, the home of Xander Coppermane. Inside, a distant masculine figure struggles against an unforgiving blizzard. Gomruss examines the angles from which Nox is reflected and is able to trace the snowy landscape as it jumps between shards. With that knowledge, he calculates when Nox will next appear and for how long. On his mark, the party jump through the portal together!

### Nox

Cold air hits the party like a brick wall. They had dressed for hot weather! Bantonio wraps himself in his new smart-mouthed fur cloak. Worryingly, the portal closes behind them. Gomruss calls Xander's name and the figure turns to approach. Xander Coppermane brushes the frost from his full, ginger beard and embraces Dayal. Despite his obvious confusion, the handsome human continues to beam in his customary roguish manner. That is until Dayal berates him for trapping the expedition here. Xander retreats momentarily, concerned the party might be deceiving him. Gomruss summons the adamantine fortress so they might hear Xander's story in the warmth.

> "So you're here to rescue me? Sensational! But first, you must help me rescue my ex-wife. She's still out there somewhere. Trapped, like me."
> 
> *—Xander*

Xander explains how he first met Ylva One-Felled when she was separated from her foraging party by a sudden blizzard. He found her trying to fend off a pack of winter wolves and swiftly came to her rescue. Together, the pair sought shelter in a nearby cave and were found the next morning. At first, Xander thought the staff had returned him to that moment, only this time his ex-wife remained forever out of reach.

To Gomruss, something seems familiar about Xander's story. Conjuring images of the daring rescue in his mind, Gomruss jolts when the blizzard suddenly becomes a sandstorm. Ahead, a feminine figure weakens trying to fend off a pack of phase spiders. Gomruss recalls casting a wall of fire to exterminate the creatures! He rushes to the young woman's aid, offering her water. She collapses into his arms.

> "Gomruss? What's wrong?"
> 
> "I remember... meeting someone. Her name was Isma."
> 
> *—Firien to Gomruss*

When questioned, Xander laments that his lust for adventure clashed with his ex-wife's sense of duty. Though he still loved her, he decided to leave Nox. Bantonio commiserates with Xander and the pair share stories of their paramours. Bantonio suggests that rescuing Ylva might restore the portal. On the other hand, Arti encourages Xander to let her go. To escape his cell, he must allow it to torture him no longer. Xander glances once more to the silhouette of his ex-wife in the blizzard, now closer than ever as if tempting him. He offers Arti a nod of thanks before the portal reappears.

### Plane of Mirrors

Returning to the gateway, Xander looks around in awe. Further ahead, Gomruss considers the similarity of his and Xander's memories. Could this be a deception? Or was it simply a coincidence? He then spies a beautiful Antiquan landscape reflected in a large glass shard. Dayal, having studied Doctor Darshan's books, realises the setting might relate to one of his memories. Together, the party step through the portal and find themselves attending a traditional Antiquan wedding.

### Primal Antiqua

A pink tree blossom lands on Gomruss' nose as he surveys the scene. A red carpet separates two sections of seated wedding guests. Up ahead, Doctor Gurdeep Darhshan stands opposite a red-veiled woman. Behind them, a romantic beach bleeds into the horizon and the mountains beyond. Arti and Bantonio take seats beside the guests and find their abstract behaviour odd. Dayal creeps up beside Gurdeep. The doctor ignores them, staring absently into the eyes of his veiled wife. A charm!

As Dayal prepares a spell, the veiled figure turns to threaten them. Bantonio readies his sword as Dayal clicks their fingers. Doctor Darshan blinks back to consciousness and yells when his betrothed rises into the air! Her red veil tears into tentacle-like ribbons, revealing the nightmarish face of a banshee. The wedding guests spring from their seats and burst, leaving mirror mephits where they stood. Bantonio leaps into action, striking the banshee four times in quick succession with his sword of sharpness. The banshee screams as its ethereal body is cut to shreds.

> "There will be no honeymoon!"
> 
> *—Bantonio*

The mirror mephits swarm and attack the party with their obsidian beaks. Fortunately, they pose little threat. Their glass bodies shatter easily when struck with a bludgeoning weapon. One mephit stubs its beak trying to pierce Firien's armour. The dragonborn retaliates with a fatal blow, causing the mephit to explode!
