Blythe opts to stay behind with Roshia. She warns that a mirror counterpart to each of the party will spawn somewhere in the Plane of Mirrors once they cross the threshold. Dayal offers to join the party; they should be able to distinguish between the expedition members and their counterparts. Gomruss approaches the portal to see a reflection of himself and his friends. He reaches out, touching his fingertips to those of his reflection, only to find the glass is permeable. With a deep breath, the Six of Steel step through the portal.

### Plane of Mirrors

The path ahead is almost imperceptible. All around, an infinite undulating web of glass shards reflects thousands of locations, as though the party were inside a shattered mirror. Behind them, the vault doors close and disappear. When questioned, Darkhalijz explains that his staff could not only banish his enemies to the Plane of Mirrors, but deposit them in a cell fashioned from their most torturous memories. When Darkhalijz admits that he never figured out how to release his victims, Firien grips his throat, silencing him.

Moving forward, Arti spots the Miasma reflected in a nearby glass shard. Approaching carefully, they equip their lantern flail. When the Miasma next appears, it is dispersed by the reflection of Arti's lantern. Arti sings Saint Lydia's song. As they suspected, a distant beacon of light illuminates the Miasma. Another pillar! Firien takes note of the volcanic terrain beyond the threshold. Could this portal lead to Discidium? The pair agree to return here once the expedition are safe.

The glass shards become larger as the party stray further from the vault doors. Up ahead, Dayal spots a reflection of Nox, Xander's home. Inside, a distant figure struggles against a blizzard.
