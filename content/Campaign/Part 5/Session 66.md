Aequor addresses the Six of Steel in a calm, feminine voice. She claims that the second pillar lies beyond the Miasma to the south-east. Firien thumbs their chin. Activating the pillar would reveal the obscured half of Mallia, allowing the Mallians to expand their territory southward, away from the Manyshells. When questioned, Aequor assures the party they will be protected. The gargantuan dragon reveals a lure attached to her dorsal fin. It begins to glow, enveloping the airship in radiant light, much like the Verity's shield.

> "Well, I'm up for one last adventure."
>
> *—Guy Brush*

After some deliberation, the Six of Steel agree to the detour. Guy helms the airship, following Aequor closely. The fog begins to swirl at the apex of her fins, catching the airship in a slipstream. Within seconds, the white void turns purple as the airship is transported beyond the Miasma. Up ahead, an earthmote enters the radiant shield. Using his spyglass, Guy spots a cenote at the centre of a withered jungle. Gelatinous vines drape over the edge of the sinkhole, making it look like the mouth of a giant lamprey.

### Second Pillar

Using the airship's rope ladder, the Six of Steel, Elaina, Cylaros and Lothran descend into the cenote. Unfurling their wings, Arti circles the cavern to check for threats. The pillar stands in shallow, stagnant waters directly below them. Gomruss approaches and carefully scrapes away the algae to reveal the pillar's inscriptions. It was another verse of Saint Lydia's Song! When Arti's fingertips touch the pillar, Ten's hand appears beside theirs. Together, the pair are transported to a moment in Ten's past.

> "You're not a coward cos you cower  
> You're brave because they broke you  
> Yet broken still you breathe  
> So breathe, breathe just breathe."
>
> *—Saint Lydia's Song*

### Anfang

The angered cries of a hundred people suddenly assault Arti's senses. Pushing through the mob, they find themselves outside a city they recognise as Caelum's Landing. Looking to the horizon, Arti realises the Miasma and earthmotes are missing! A thug beside Arti throws a rock and a grunt of pain captures Arti's attention. Stood beside a different pillar, Ten tries desperately to address the mob. Ten appears young, whole. Where before their long, pitch black hair obscured a bisected countenance of green and purple, short locks instead suit a half-crown of green crystals.

> "Please, you must listen! Chauntea has given me a task. There is going to be a terrible blight on this land. Her pillars are our only means of protection!"
>
> *—Ten beseeches the mob*

Another rock strikes Ten's head and they stumble backward. Sensing an opportunity, several of the mob charge forward and take sledgehammers to the pillar. As Ten's consciousness fades, so does Arti's. When next they wake, Arti finds themself looking up at a proud golden dragonborn—a cleric of Bahamut. When Arti reaches up to take the cleric's hand, they do so with Ten's hand. The dragonborn is joined by a quiet elf druid whose garb bears the floral blessing of Chauntea.

> "My name is Raikhan, this is my partner Syl. We believe you."
>
> *—Raikhan helps Ten*

### Second Pillar

Arti's touch illuminates the first of the pillar's four faces. Firien invites Elaina, Cylaros and Lothran to complete the ritual. The trio step forward to receive Ten's memory. At their touch, a wave of energy cascades over the earthmote and beyond. The Feywild Ones return to consciousness just as a sunbeam pierces the gloom above the skylight. A glorious landscape greets the party as they climb out of the cenote. A sudden cloudburst creates a rainbow across the vibrant Mallian jungle. Firien breathes deeply, letting the cool rain seep between their scales. They whisper a sending to the yuan-ti they met at Farwest.
