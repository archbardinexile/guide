Aequor addresses the Six of Steel in a calm, feminine voice. She claims that the second pillar lies beyond the Miasma to the south-east. Firien thumbs their chin. Activating the pillar would reveal the obscured half of Mallia, allowing the Mallians to expand their territory southward, away from the Manyshells. When questioned, Aequor assures the party they will be protected. The gargantuan dragon reveals a lure attached to her dorsal fin. It begins to glow, enveloping the airship in radiant light, much like the Verity's shield.

After some deliberation, the Six of Steel agree to the detour. Guy helms the airship, following Aequor closely. The fog begins to swirl at the apex of her fins, catching the airship in a slipstream. Within seconds, the white void turns purple as the airship is transported beyond the Miasma. Up ahead, an earthmote enters the radiant shield. Using his spyglass, Guy spots a cenote at the centre of a withered jungle. Gelatinous vines drape over the edge of the sinkhole, making it look like the mouth of a giant gastropod.

Using the airship's rope ladder, the Six of Steel, Elaina, Cylaros and Lothran descend into the cenote.
