### Roshia Cralee

Sea-elf graviturge, Gomruss' old mentor

- **982 PO** Aged 19, Gomruss invents the game of *Dice Monarchs* and spends 4 years developing the rules.

- **986 PO** Aged 23, Gomruss travels to *Ruby* and enters into a 10-year contract with *River Chapman*, a game publisher at *Resin Curios Inc.* Gomruss offers Chapman publishing rights and agrees to pay for the game's creation out of his share of the profits.

- **988 PO** Aged 25, Gomruss enrols in *Caelum's Landing University* to study graviturgy. There he meets Professor *Roshia Cralee*.

- **990 PO** Aged 27, Gomruss is expelled from CLU for... He later finds employment with the *Commission* but is dismissed for accidentally colliding two earthmotes.

- **991 PO** Aged 28, Gomruss travels to *Arx* with Roshia. He finds employment unearthing ruins for the *Arx Institute of Archaeology*. Gomruss wakes in a tavern to find himself sharing a bed with a dead body, apparently his wife. Documents of their association are found to be forgeries and Gomruss is exonerated.

- **996 PO** Aged 33, Gomruss' contract with Chapman ends. When thugs begin to harass him, he discovers Resin Curios Inc. has been selling Dice Monarchs under another name, thus stealing his share of the profits. Gomruss joins the *Six of Steel* and embarks on a mission into the Miasma.

Gomruss' wife was actually, Isma, a former servant to *Abhishek*. Having built a friendship with Gomruss, she requested he pose as her husband to nullify Abishek's claim to her. When Abishek's cronies came to kidnap her, she was killed. The cronies fled, leaving Gomruss bound and silenced.
