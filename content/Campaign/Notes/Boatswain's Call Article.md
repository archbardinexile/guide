*An extract from the Boatswain's Call newspaper dated 996, Flavum 21st, Sunday.*

**The Flagship Returns!**

Ten days ago, in an unprecedented act of collaboration, a combined fleet of Free Trade Navy and Church ships engaged notorious pirate captain Bhaldrom Anchorheart in the Farwest Archipelago. Since the fleet's safe return, Vice Admiral Kilik's lacking statement on the incident has bred rumours concerning the urgency of the deployment. Though the recapture of the Navy's flagship would seem to indicate Anchorheart's defeat, Kilik neither confirmed nor denied Anchorheart's death. In fact, the fleet returned unscathed and with their full complement of cannonballs.

Many speculate that the incident was orchestrated by the Mallians, though proof of this has yet to be revealed. What is certain is that the fleet's commander, Captain Elyse Trelawney, is to receive a commendation and command of the Aubrey for her outstanding initiative in assembling the fleet—despite her public reprimand of Kilik's apparent inaction. Furthermore, known Umberlant ships have been observed raising independent flags instead of the bitch queen's colours. This may be evidence of a power vacuum in the Saltmoot following Anchorheart's departure.
