Led by Chief Constable [[Rosalind Armbruster]], the Inland Guard is the public police service headquartered at Caelum's Landing. Funded by the [[The Ministry|Ministry]], the greater number of guards compared to the [[Church of Caelum]]'s Clerics allows for more efficient policing of [[Fundus]] and its neighbouring [[Earthmotes|earthmotes]].
