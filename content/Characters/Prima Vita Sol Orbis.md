Prima Vita Sol Orbis was the first and only child of [[Caelum Sol Orbis]]. She is hailed as the first Exarch and progenitor of more than thirty generations of the Sol Orbis family. Born on the 4th day of Niveus 22 AO, her benevolent reign would begin at only 48 years of age. Initially hesitant to shoulder the burdens of ruling a nascent theocracy, Prima Vita found courage in the counsel of [[Alberich Geboren]], King [[Anfarwol]]'s last son. The two married in 26 PO. Her reign would last 85 years until her passing in 111 PO.

Prima Vita is described as being ethereally beautiful with delicate features. Statues often depict her with her hands cradled in front of her chest in a reserved manner. Aasimar descended from Prima Vita often bear Caelum's distinctive silver halo on their foreheads. This has become the emblem of the [Church of Caelum](factions/church-of-caelum).
