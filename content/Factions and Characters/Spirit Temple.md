The Spirit Temple on [[Fundus]]' north-western shore is the isolated home of a clique of monks who seek a deep connection to nature through the study of wild beasts and their associated elements. This allows them to terraform new lands and arrest dangerous rogue [[Fio#Earthmotes|earthmotes]]. The monks work closely with the [[Church of Caelum]] when earthmotes threaten to collide with allied territories, though they do not worship Caelum.

###### Notable Members

- **Baihu**, Master Monk of the Spirit Temple.
- **Keijo**, Scarred half-elf with the spirit of a tiger.
- **Blue-Belly**, Chameleon lizardfolk monk.
