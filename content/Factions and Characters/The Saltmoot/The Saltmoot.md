The Saltmoot is a loose alliance of pirates, privateers and merchant crews governed by those with the largest fleets and the greatest wealth. The city state of [[Libertalia]] serves as headquarters for the ever-changing members of a pirate conclave that keep the various guilds of the [[Manyshells|Manyshells]] in check.
