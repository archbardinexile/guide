Anfarwol was an Eladrin from the *Feywild* whose appearance would change depending on the season, reflecting his self-proclaimed immortality. Anfarwol arrived on [[Fio]] via a portal of his own creation c. one thousand years before the [[index#The Rising|Rising]]. His supernatural influence on the land would make it bountiful, causing flora and fauna from the Feywild to emerge. This land would eventually become known as the [[Sylvan Kingdoms]].

Anfarwol discovered [[Caelum's Landing|Anfang]] and imposed his order on the disparate human societies. Enslaved by the immortal elf, the humans would spend almost a century carving the citadel of [[Fundus|Caer Wraidd]] into the [[Fundus|Asgwrn Mountains]]. Despite the hardship, the citadel's construction would bring prosperity and unity to the human societies. The humans would name Anfarwol the immortal elf-king of Anfang. Anfarwol was murdered during the sacking of Caer Wraidd in 26 PO when Caelumites demanded he abdicate and recognise [[Prima Vita Sol Orbis]] as their new theocratic leader.
