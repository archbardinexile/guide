Little is known of the fiend Tenebrae. Restricted chronicles held deep within the [[Caelum's Landing#The Oratory|Oratory]]'s vaults make reference to them only as the shadowy being behind the construction of [[Mundi Eversor]]. The [[Insequentes Umbra]], a unique painting also held in the vaults, depicts them as a cambion standing upon the shoulder of Eversor, wielding a hellish flaming greatsword. The figure is depicted in silhouette with large barbed horns and dragon-like wings. Some scholars dispute whether this depiction of Tenebrae is accurate.
