Often misnomered a cult, the Crosscut Circle are a sinister but peaceful protest organisation that wish to expose the hypocrisy of the [[Church of Caelum]]. They claim the [[Fio#The Miasma|Miasma]] was released one thousand years ago as a result of [[Caelum Sol Orbis]]' ritual to expel the darkness within him so that he might to ascend to divinity. They also claim [[Mundi Eversor]] was created by [[Tenebrae]] and his followers to stop Caelum.
