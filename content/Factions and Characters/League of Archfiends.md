The League of Archfiends is said to be a secret cabal of fiends who operate from within the [[Miasma]], subsidising illegal practices across the [[The Relief/index|Relief]] that will bring them luxury in their empire beyond the border. It is rumoured they even conspired with [[Tenebrae]] to create [[Mundi Eversor]] 1,000 years ago, but it has never been proven the organisation exists or ever existed.
