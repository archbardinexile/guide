The Dawnroot Druids make their home in the [[Fundus#Dawnroot Forest|Dawnroot Forest]] on [[Fundus]], secluded from the trappings of lifestyle in the capital. They revere and protect a portal to the elemental plane of water, the source of the [[Fundus|River Fountainhead]], which they believe to be a blessing from *Silvanus*, the deity of wild nature. Though they disapprove of the [[Church of Caelum]]'s expansionist practices, they prefer not to intervene.

###### Notable Members

- **Hlynur**, stoic, silver-haired male high elf with antlers; leader of the clan
- **Myrthe**, headstrong, dark-skinned female centaur; deputy leader
- **Falvalor**, young and trusting moon elf
