- **Race:** Aasimar, Female
- **Class:** Paladin (Oath of Redemption)
- **Background:** Exarch, Prefect of Caelum's Landing
- **Born:** 964 PO, Caelum's Landing

Aura Sol Orbis is the Exarch of the [[Church of Caelum]]. She has warm-tinted brown skin and solid white eyes much like her father, and sports a long afro gathered into a high ponytail. She and her siblings bare the silver Sol Orbis halo on their foreheads. Those who are granted audience with Aura see her as a kind and understanding soul, while those closest to her know her to be solemn and somewhat lonesome.

Aura Sol Orbis was born unto [[Ardeat Sol Orbis]] and his first wife on the 14th day of Viridis in the [[Caelum's Landing#The Oratory|Oratory]] on [[Caelum's Landing]]. Much of her upbringing was focused on preparing for the day she would inevitably take over as Exarch. Though she shared a close relationship with her father, her politics would grow apart from his as she watched him perpetuate the [[The Medusae Wars|Medusae Wars]] and the [[Church of Caelum]]'s expansionist practices.

Following the death of her father, Aura Sol Orbis was anointed Exarch on the 11th day of Flavum. Accepting her crown at only 22 years of age, Aura took a paladin's oath of redemption, swearing to right the wrongs of the the Medusae Wars and help those affected reintegrate into society.

Aura would go on to declare her brother, [[Spiro Sol Orbis]], Grand Paladin and Prefect of Ingenium, her sibling, [[Sana Sol Orbis]], Prefect of Madanau and her sister [[Clara Sol Orbis]], Prefect of [[Tenebrae's Refuge]].
