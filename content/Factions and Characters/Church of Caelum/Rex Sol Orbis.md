###### Summary

Rex Sol Orbis was the descendant of [[Pluvia and Nebula Sol Orbis|Nebula Sol Orbis]] and the second Exarch of the second Sol Orbis dynasty. Contrary to his cousin, [[Cor Sol Orbis]], Rex is described as aggressive, manipulative, even irrational in the waning days of his reign. Though he was defeated by Cor in 203 PO, ending the War of Accession, he would go on to become Exarch when his cousin died exactly ten years later. Rex became the founder of the [[Kingdom of Diamante]] following his invasion of [[Atada and Atado]] in 237 PO, and was deposed and executed by republican rebels in 274 PO.

#### The Diamond Crusades (237–241 PO)

Intense feuds between the fractured nations of [[Atada and Atado]] resulted in the deaths of several Church missionaries, prompting Exarch Rex Sol Orbis to declare a holy war on the twinned earthmotes. Allied with King [[Dimas Fantoccio]] of the northern realms, Rex Sol Orbis was able to unite the nations of Atada and Atado in only four years. Inspired by the collective wealth of his new kingdom, Rex installed Fantoccio as puppet-king of the newly proclaimed [[Kingdom of Diamante]].

#### The First Fundus Civil War (266–274 PO)

In 266 PO, when a series of public incidents exemplified the Church's oppressive form of government, a human named [[Anselm Chlodovech]] took it upon himself to deliver the people's demands to Rex Sol Orbis and his family. When Rex laughed Chlodovech out of the [[Caelum's Landing#The Oratory|Oratory]], the aspiring politician declared war. In his arrogance, Rex allowed Chlodovech to leave. The ensuing eight-year civil war concluded with a civilian march on the Oratory. When Rex Sol Orbis refused to leave his throne, Chlodovech drove a spear through his heart, pinning him to the throne.

Following Rex's execution, Chlodovech would declare himself Chancellor of the nascent [[Church of Caelum|Fundus Republic]] and construction began on the [[Caelum's Landing#Ministerial Houses|Ministerial Houses]] where his new government would take shape. A period of unrest would follow as Chlodovech's Republic consistently failed to deliver the needs of its people.
