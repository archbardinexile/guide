Cor Sol Orbis was the descendant of [[Pluvia and Nebula Sol Orbis|Pluvia Sol Orbis]] and the first Exarch of the second Sol Orbis dynasty. Cor is described as being warm and compassionate, showing resolute mercy even in the heat of battle. He and [[Rex Sol Orbis]] would return to [[Caelum's Landing]] in 196 PO to lay claim to the exarchy. The splintered [[Factions and Characters/Church of Caelum/index|Church of Caelum]] would split into two factions, each supporting an heir, beginning the War of Accession. Seven years later, Cor defeated Rex at the Battle of [[Dawnroot Forest|Fountainhead Falls]] and chose to spare his life. The newly crowned Exarch would go on to declare his cousin Grand Paladin.

In 204 PO, Cor would to send aid to [[Madanau]] after its perilous arrival on [[Fio]]. When he was met with adversity, Rex advised annexing the territory to further his expansionist goals. Fortunately, Cor declined, and instead sought the help of [[Lydia Iremia]], a healer from [[The Relief/Montanum/index|Montanum]]. Together, the pair convinced Madanau's proud denizens to accept Church aid via construction of an embassy.

Cor's failing health over a decade would lead to his death in 213 PO. In that time, Lydia served as the Exarch's nurse and the pair fell in love. A marriage ceremony was held in Cor's bedchamber just before his death. It was the Exarch's hope that Lydia might better influence Rex from a position of legal power, once he was anointed the new Exarch.
