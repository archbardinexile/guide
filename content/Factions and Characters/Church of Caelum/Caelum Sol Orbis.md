Caelum Sol Orbis was an aasimar born from human parentage on the 24th day of Ascendentem 64 AO in the city of [[Caelum's Landing|Anfang]], now known as [[Caelum's Landing]]. Raised by a religious family, Caelum quickly gained renown as a paladin of *Lathander*. His generosity and exploits protecting Anfang justified his rise to power in Lathander's church. The [[index#The Rising|Ascensus in Caelum]] chronicles Caelum's struggle against [[Mundi Eversor]] and his ascent to divinity.

Caelum is described as being quiet and kindly but fiercely determined and dutiful. He had warm-tinted brown skin, white eyes, and is depicted sporting hair ranging from a buzz cut to short afro curls. Caelum had a distinctive silver halo on his forehead as a result of his aasimar heritage. The halo was passed down to his daughter, [[Prima Vita Sol Orbis]] and all her descendants. The halo has become the emblem of the [[Church of Caelum]]. The first of Lathander's church to be given the title *Grand Paladin*, Caelum was tasked with protecting Anfang from the colossus Mundi Eversor in 0 PO, which would eventually lead to his ascension and the [[index#The Rising|Rising]].
