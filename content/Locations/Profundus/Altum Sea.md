The Altum Sea stretches far into the uncharted regions of the [[The Miasma|Miasma]]. As a result, most denizens of the [[The Relief|Relief]] tend to have at least a rudimentary understanding of seafaring or aviation. The destruction of [[Mundi Eversor]] wrought great storms that filled the cavities left behind after the [[The Rising|Rising]], leading many aquatic races to take refuge in its depths.

The largest congregation of sea-folk in the Relief resides at the bottom of the Vena Trench. This mysterious underwater city has been named [[Abyssus]] by explorers that have until recently only inferred its existence. Not much is known about the city, other than it lies west of [[Mundi Eversor]].
