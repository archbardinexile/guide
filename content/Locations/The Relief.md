The Relief is a circular expanse in [[Fio]]'s northern hemisphere unaffected by the [[The Miasma|Miasma]]. Approximately 2,000 miles in diameter, the Relief spans six unique regions with an abundance of culture that transcends even the barriers between the planes of existence.
